Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

When you sign up for one of our free gifts, we collect your first name (optional) and your email address. That's all.

What we use it for

We use your email to send you the gift you asked for and, from time to time, short notes about our books, such as a new release or a free early copy for readers. We never sell or rent your information to anyone.

Where it's stored

Our email list is managed with Kit (kit.com), a mail service that stores your name and email on our behalf. Their privacy policy is available on their website.

Your choices

Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. One click and you're off the list. You can also write to us at any time to ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted.

This website

This site doesn't use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.
